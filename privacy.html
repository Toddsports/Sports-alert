Privacy Policy

Sports Alert allows users to subscribe to receive SMS sports alerts.

Users voluntarily provide their mobile phone number through the Sports Alert signup page and must explicitly consent to receive SMS messages before any messages are sent.

Message frequency varies depending on the teams selected and game activity.

Message and data rates may apply.

You may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages or HELP for assistance.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Your phone number is used solely to deliver the sports alert messages you requested.
